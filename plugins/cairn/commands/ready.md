Run `cn ready $ARGUMENTS`. Show the lines as they were printed, so the reference form,
`app-14 "fix connection retry"`, is not shortened; shortening it makes the list
unreadable a day later. Then say what each one is: `cn show <id>` for every row, and
under its line a sentence of what the work is and where it stands, read from the brief
rather than invented, and why it is next — its priority, and that nothing holds it. A
row marked `· needs ios` is work this session cannot finish, not work to hide: name it
the same way, and say what it needs.

If it prints nothing, say the ready list is empty and stop.
